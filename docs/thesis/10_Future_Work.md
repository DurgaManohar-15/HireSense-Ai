# Chapter 10: Future Research & Development

## 10.1 Planned Extensions
1. **Isolated Multi-Language Coding Sandbox:** Implement a containerized sandbox execution engine (Docker / gVisor) supporting Java, Python, C++, and Go with memory, CPU, and syscall limits.
2. **Multimodal Audio/Video Interview Analysis:** Integrate WebRTC audio feature extraction to assess vocal clarity, pacing, and confidence during mock interviews.
3. **Institutional ATS Integration:** Provide direct webhooks and export integrations into campus placement ERPs.
4. **Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT):** Transition static skill mastery thresholds into dynamic probabilistic Bayesian knowledge tracing models as student attempt histories expand.

# Chapter 1: Introduction

## 1.1 Context and Motivation
In contemporary higher education, engineering students preparing for campus recruitment face significant challenges in accurately assessing their readiness across multiple competitive dimensions. Traditional campus recruitment drives assess candidates on a comprehensive suite of proficiencies:
1. Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning
2. Core Computer Science and Technical Knowledge (Data Structures, Algorithms, DBMS, Operating Systems)
3. Resume Parsability and Technical Depth (ATS alignment)
4. Conversational and Technical Communication in Structured Interviews

Currently, students lack an integrated, objective, and explainable diagnostic platform that aggregates multi-source performance data into a unified, reproducible readiness metric.

## 1.2 Objectives of HireSense AI
The primary research and engineering objectives of **HireSense AI** are:
1. **Explainable Readiness Assessment:** Design and implement a deterministic **Placement Readiness Score (PRS)** that dynamically integrates multi-source evidence without relying on opaque, ungrounded Large Language Model (LLM) scoring.
2. **Multi-Source Evidence Calibration:** Reconcile demonstrated candidate competencies across timed diagnostic assessments, ATS resume parsing, and interactive mock interview transcripts.
3. **Adaptive Preparation Planning:** Automatically generate personalized, day-by-day learning roadmaps that prioritize candidate-specific skill gaps against platform-configured company benchmarks.
4. **Academic Evaluation Infrastructure:** Provide empirical measurement tools—including Normalized Learning Gain ($g$) and standardized effect sizes (Cohen's $d$)—to support reproducible educational research.

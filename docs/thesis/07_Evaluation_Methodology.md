# Chapter 7: Evaluation Methodology & Statistical Framework

## 7.1 Experimental Design
To evaluate the pedagogical impact of the HireSense AI platform during student pilot testing, a randomized controlled trial (RCT) or quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group design is formulated:
* **Intervention Group (Group A):** Students utilize HireSense AI's adaptive preparation roadmaps, diagnostic assessments, and AI mock interviews.
* **Control Group (Group B):** Students prepare using conventional static study materials without personalized readiness guidance.

## 7.2 Quantitative Statistical Metrics
1. **Absolute Score Gain ($\Delta$):**
   $$\Delta = \bar{X}_{\text{post}} - \bar{X}_{\text{pre}}$$
2. **Relative Percentage Gain ($\% \text{Gain}$):**
   $$\% \text{Gain} = \left( \frac{\bar{X}_{\text{post}} - \bar{X}_{\text{pre}}}{\bar{X}_{\text{pre}}} \right) \times 100\%$$
3. **Richard Hake's Normalized Learning Gain ($g$):**
   $$g = \frac{\bar{X}_{\text{post}} - \bar{X}_{\text{pre}}}{100 - \bar{X}_{\text{pre}}}$$
4. **Standardized Effect Size (Cohen's $d$):**
   $$d = \frac{\mu_{\text{post}} - \mu_{\text{pre}}}{\sigma_{\text{pooled}}} \quad \text{where} \quad \sigma_{\text{pooled}} = \sqrt{\frac{(N_1 - 1)\sigma_1^2 + (N_2 - 1)\sigma_2^2}{N_1 + N_2 - 2}}$$
